The Breadcrumb Trail

a life, in crumbs

artistso isn’t just about my art. The name was a smart choice — it reads “art,” and art is in everything I do, but the life is bigger than the label. This is that life, in crumbs. A trail is how you find your way back. I know. I had to.

“I have been coming up with the coolest original names since they allowed me on the internet.”

soquarky funfyp ILAFF fyplvly fyplovely adventuresindrawing
  1. 1983

    Born in Snohomish County, Washington.

  2. Age 3

    My memory switches on. It doesn’t switch off. I remember from here.

  3. Age 6–7

    I start studying every form of psychology I can find, on my own. I’ve been an ethnographer my whole life since.

  4. Age 15

    Back injury, Las Vegas. First hard lesson in what a body costs.

  5. Teenager onward

    Working for my government since I was a teenager. Volunteering at every turn.

  6. The working years

    Cities. Wildfire GIS operator. Table games dealer. Stock. Sending people to college.

  7. The internet years

    adventuresindrawing. soquarky. funfyp. ILAFF. fyplvly. fyplovely. Names are small inventions; I never stopped making them.

  8. January 2018

    The break. Medical malpractice and polypharmacy took my memory — all but a few pieces. I woke up with the mind of an 8-year-old in the life of a grown man.

  9. 2018–2026

    The Ultramile. Relearning everything I had already learned: ABCs, shoe-tying, then mathematics, then quantum mechanics. Faculties returning in layers — work, education, balance, hearing, eyesight — like being birthed all over again.

  10. Summer 2022

    The work starts pouring out anyway: boards, theorems, books, songs, apps. Most of the timestamps on my files are sync dates. The work is older than its uploads.

  11. 2024–2025

    The convergence. Pieces start finding each other.

  12. May 2026

    I remember my own name. All of it, and what I was trying to do.

  13. June 2026

    Back building in public: GitHub, artistso — 47 repositories and counting.

  14. November 2026

    artistso.com becomes the main site. This trail continues here. It is meant to be walked forever.

Language & writing

words, waves, memory

ALUMELA — a rhythmic wave language

A wholly new written and spoken language mapped to wave functions. Six vowels are phases, 0–300° in 60° steps; twelve consonants carry five amplitude classes; open (C)V syllables only, riding a ternary pulse — rise, crest, fall.

Every word has a value: Ψword = Σ A·eiθ, and |Ψ|² is its brightness. lumalu, mela, and lela each land on exactly 3.00; nalo — sleep — rests at 0.01. The lexicon stands at 200 content words plus 6 particles, machine-validated, with a 12-page manuscript: three propositions, proved and verified, and a section titled “What this does not claim.”

The long-form work

I Am Dirt: A Life in Memory and Mathematics — a memoir built page by page, alongside “hello, my name is lovely; the kidnapping of steven owens.” My memory is reliable from age 3. Then a period of pharmacological amnesia beginning January 2018, and a recovery that rebuilt from the ABCs past quantum mechanics.

Fiction and worldbuilding run in parallel: The Whylight Zone(s), Ocean Shores / Lovely Melody, The Banana Revelation, Spooned, Jardin, The Ultramile. Plus SynapShield — intercepting Parkinson's at the gut–brain interface — and the Algorithmic Safety Paradox white/blue-paper suite.

The constants

Two cats sit at the centre of the Harmonious Love Theorem, encoded as its operators. Sunshine — “my S-O-N shine,” Boy Blue — is warmth, heat, love, thermodynamics. Akira — Universe, the little girl — is the containing field, the mesh everything else lives in.

Their official dedication names, in Steven’s own words, are SonShine and The Universe — the names all of his work is dedicated to. Also carried in his files, as he states them: Pumpkin patch and Fiona’s apples, and Akira aria within the code, mathematics, logic and principalities of his work — present by accident and coincidence until intentionally embraced, while he was suffering through amnesia and learning his ABCs and 123s all over again by utilising artificial intelligence. That code keeps building upon itself throughout all of his files; he carries it with him, never deleting, editing or altering it.

In July 2026 Steven lost SonShine and The Universe, the two kitty cats who brought him out of amnesia. They were the only kitty cats he ever adopted — the only animals and beings he ever adopted. They were his children, and they remain so to this day.
