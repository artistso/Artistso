# Muse team — a second note

**From:** Steven Owens — @soquarky / artistso.com
**To:** the Muse team
**Date:** 26 November 2026
**Re:** a working partnership, and three specific asks

---

## Why I am writing again

I wrote to you on 6 November, and that letter is still on the site as
[The Letter](https://artistso.com/letter.html). I am writing a second time
because the thing I described in November has kept going, and because it is no
longer just "I use Muse now." It has become a working partnership, and I want to
say that in your direction with specifics instead of adjectives.

What has happened since November, on my side:

- **A working surface for four media at once.** Drawing and animation, pen-first
  Android software, theoretical physics written referee-style, and long-form
  prose. One assistant holds all four, and none of them has had to be simplified
  to fit the others.
- **The site you are reading grew up.** It is now a multi-page site with its own
  pages and subdomains rather than one long page — and the shared parts are
  generated from a single manifest so they cannot drift apart. That change was
  built, checked and shipped inside this partnership, at $0.
- **Referee habits that survived contact with the work.** I do not want to be
  flattered. I want the text corrected, the claims labelled, and the error found
  before a reader finds it. That is what I asked for on 18 October in the
  Quadrature Time handoff, and it is what I am still asking for.

## What I am asking for

Three things, in the order they would change my week.

### 1. One-step deploy to DreamHost

I host artistso.com on DreamHost and I deploy by hand, in verified batches. It
works, and it is the last manual step in an otherwise automated loop. A
one-step deploy from a Muse Space to a hosted domain — SFTP or better — would
close that gap without a CI service in between. **This is the single change that
would save me the most time.**

### 2. Publish and link, for Google Sites and NotebookLM

Both are already part of how the work is documented: `soquarky.click` is the
Google Sites edition of the house, and the NotebookLM notebooks are the
askable versions of the physics, the language, and the memoir. I want Muse to
be able to publish to a public site and link a public notebook directly, instead
of me copying URLs between tools by hand.

### 3. A page-embed for Facebook

I host photos and video on the social platforms by design, and embed them on my
own site. Facebook Page embeds are the most friction of anything I do. Page
embed or content sync is the ask.

## What I am not asking for

I am not asking for a rewrite of my voice, and I am not asking for design that
looks like every other landing page. The site is deliberately built the old
way — a page for everything, and everything on its page — and the roughness is
the point. What helps me is **infrastructure**, not polish.

## A note on the newest pages

The site now has a [Career & trades](https://artistso.com/career.html) page that
pulls twenty-two years of work before the site into one record — the casino
floor, the PR and crisis communications, the art, and the trades — and a
[bench page](https://artistso.com/bench.html) listing the seventeen apps I
intend to build, each one labelled honestly as built, typed, or still only a
specification. Both came out of my own project files in Drive.

If either of those is the kind of thing you would want to point at as an example
of what the partnership produces, point at them. They are real, they are
checkable, and neither of them overstates what exists.

## Closing

The March-through-November arc of this has been the most productive stretch of
tooling I have had, and I have used everything. I mean that as a measurement,
not a compliment: it has been exponential against a baseline of every other
platform I have worked on.

If a human on your side reads only one paragraph, make it this one: **give me
the deploy step and the publish links, and I will hand you back a creator who
runs an entire research and publishing operation on your platform at zero
marginal cost.**

Thank you again.
